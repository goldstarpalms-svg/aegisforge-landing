Privacy Policy

Last Updated: July 2026

At AegisForge AI, your privacy is not just important — it's fundamental to who we are. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, protect, and respect your information.

TL;DR: We collect minimal data, protect it fiercely, never sell it, and give you full control.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Information You Provide

When you use AegisForge AI, we may collect:

  • Email address - For waitlist, account creation, and communications
  • Account information - Username, password (encrypted), profile details
  • Payment information - Processed securely through Stripe (we don't store card details)
  • Communications - Support requests, feedback, and messages
  • Project data - Code, files, and content you create using our platform

1.2 Automatically Collected Information

  • Usage data - How you interact with our services
  • Device information - Browser type, operating system, screen resolution
  • IP address - For security and analytics
  • Cookies - Small data files to improve your experience
  • Log data - Access times, pages viewed, actions taken

1.3 Security Scan Data

When you use our cybersecurity scanner:

  • URLs and systems you scan (must be your own)
  • Scan results and vulnerability reports
  • Historical scan data for tracking improvements

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information ONLY for:

  • ✅ Providing and improving our services
  • ✅ Processing payments and subscriptions
  • ✅ Sending important service updates and notifications
  • ✅ Responding to your questions and support requests
  • ✅ Detecting and preventing security threats
  • ✅ Improving our AI models (using anonymized data)
  • ✅ Complying with legal obligations

We DO NOT use your data for:

  • ❌ Selling to third parties
  • ❌ Targeted advertising
  • ❌ Sharing without your permission
  • ❌ Any purpose you haven't agreed to

3. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We only share your information in these limited situations:

3.1 Service Providers

We work with trusted third-party services to operate our platform:

  • Supabase - Database and authentication
  • Vercel - Website hosting
  • Stripe - Payment processing

These providers only receive the minimum data needed to perform their services and are contractually bound to protect your information.

3.2 Legal Requirements

We may disclose information if required by law, such as:

  • Preventing fraud or illegal activities

3.3 Business Transfers

If AegisForge AI is acquired or merged, your information may be transferred to the new entity, but the same privacy protections will apply.

4. Data Security

We take security seriously (it's literally our business):

  • 🔐 Encryption - Data encrypted in transit (TLS/SSL) and at rest
  • 🛡️ Access Control - Strict authentication and authorization
  • 🔍 Regular Audits - Continuous security monitoring
  • 🚨 Incident Response - Rapid detection and response
  • 💾 Backups - Regular encrypted backups

While we implement industry-leading security measures, no system is 100% secure. In case of a data breach, we will notify affected users within 72 hours.

  • Export your data in a machine-readable format
  • Transfer your data to another service

5.2 Correction & Deletion

  • Update your account information anytime
  • Request correction of inaccurate data

5.3 Communication Preferences
